About RCB:

The Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) is a franchise cricket team based in Bangalore, participating in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008 by United Spirits, RCB plays its home matches at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium. The team is known for its passionate fan base and has had notable players like Virat Kohli. As of 2025,2026 RCB won its first & second IPL title, marking a significant achievement in its history..!!
