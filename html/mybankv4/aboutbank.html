State Bank of India (SBI):

The State Bank of India is an Indian multinational public sector bank and financial services statutory body headquartered in Mumbai. It is the largest bank in India, holding a 23% market share by assets and a 25% share of total loans and deposits, serving over 500 million customers as of 2024 . SBI is also one of the largest employers in India, with nearly 250,000 employees
